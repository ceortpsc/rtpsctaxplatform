# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate professionally, respect confidentiality, avoid harassment and discrimination, and protect users and systems from harm.

Threats, intimidation, discriminatory or sexual harassment, deliberate disclosure of private information, abuse of access, retaliation, sabotage, and knowingly unsafe engineering practices are prohibited.

Concerns should be reported privately to repository or organizational leadership. Reports will be handled with confidentiality, impartial review, proportionate action, and protection against retaliation.
