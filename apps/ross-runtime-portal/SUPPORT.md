# Support Policy

## Severity

- **SEV-1:** widespread outage, confirmed compromise, or material data exposure.
- **SEV-2:** major feature unavailable without a reasonable workaround.
- **SEV-3:** degraded function with a workaround.
- **SEV-4:** general question, documentation request, or cosmetic issue.

## Required information

Provide version, environment, operating system, exact command or route, timestamp, request or trace ID, sanitized logs, reproduction steps, and expected behavior.

Do not submit secrets, taxpayer records, identity documents, or unredacted production data through public support channels.
